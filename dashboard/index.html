📑 Postmortem: [P0] INC-502 ZeroDivisionError on 100% Promo Order

Incident ID: INC-502 | Service: checkout-service (Tier-1)

Mean Time to Acknowledge (MTTA): 12 seconds | Mean Time to Resolution (MTTR): 2m 51s

1. Executive Summary

During active promotional discount traffic, Tier-1 checkout-service crashed due to an unhandled zero-division calculation on line 24 of checkout.py. SwitchGuard ingested the telemetry alert, opened an incident war room in Discord, pinpointed the root cause, synthesized a guard clause, verified with pytest (3/3 passed), and obtained human signoff from @Shubham in 2m 51s.

2. Verified Autonomous Patch Diff

--- demo-service/checkout.py
+++ demo-service/checkout.py
@@ -21,7 +21,10 @@ def calculate_order_total(items, discount_pct=0.0):
     discount_amount = round(subtotal * (discount_pct / 100.0), 2)
     net_total = round(subtotal - discount_amount, 2)
-    savings_ratio = round(subtotal / net_total, 2)
+    if net_total == 0.0:
+        savings_ratio = 1.0  # 100% discount means full savings
+    else:
+        savings_ratio = round(subtotal / net_total, 2)

3. 5-Whys Root Cause Analysis

  1. Why did checkout fail? Unhandled ZeroDivisionError in calculate_order_total.
  2. Why was net_total zero? Customers redeemed a 100% discount promotion.
  3. Why was savings_ratio dividing by net_total? Developer assumed retail orders always have net_total > 0.
  4. Why was this not caught in staging? Unit tests only tested 10% and 50% discount tiers.
  5. Why was 100% boundary test missing? Lack of automated boundary fuzzing on checkout promotional code paths.
